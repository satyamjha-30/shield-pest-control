02 — How a visit works

Four steps, start to sign-off.

Every job — from a one-off cockroach spray to a full restaurant AMC — follows the same sequence, so you always know what's next.

Inspect

We walk the site — kitchen, storage, drains, seating — and identify the pest, the entry points and the risk areas before quoting anything.

Treat

Targeted treatment using kitchen-safe products where food is involved, scheduled around your service hours wherever possible.

Document

You get a service record with product, method and date used — the same paperwork an FSSAI or hygiene inspector will ask for.

Follow up

A scheduled re-check, or a standing AMC visit, so a treated site stays a treated site — not a repeat call six weeks later.
